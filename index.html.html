Manager Challenge

Wait for your facilitator to release a scenario. At each checkpoint, read the shift carefully and discuss with your group before selecting.

At each checkpoint you will

1. See what the situation looked like before and what has changed
2. Decide if this is development or regression
3. Identify the type of change driving it
4. Diagnose the readiness level from what you observe
5. Choose the right leadership approach

1

Development or regression?

Based on the shift — which is this?

Discuss: Has the ability changed, or has the motivation and confidence shifted? Those point in different directions.
