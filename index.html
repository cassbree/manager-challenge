Performance Readiness® — facilitator reference

Two questions: Is the person performing at a sustained, acceptable level? and Are they confident, committed and/or motivated?

R1 — Unable and Insecure or Unwilling → S1
R2 — Unable but Confident or Willing → S2
R3 — Able but Insecure or Unwilling → S3
R4 — Able and Confident or Willing → S4
S1 Telling/Directing — High direction, low support. Specifics, leader-made decisions, close supervision.
S2 Selling/Coaching — High direction, high support. Explain why, two-way dialogue, reinforce improvements.
S3 Participating/Supporting — Low direction, high support. Encourage input, listen, build confidence.
S4 Delegating — Low direction, low support. Delegate, light supervision, reinforce results.
Facilitator framing — read before running

Disagreement on readiness is expected and valuable. If two groups diagnose R1 vs R2, or R2 vs R3 — that debate IS the learning. Don\'t rush to correct it. Ask groups to justify their reasoning from what they observed.

The diagnostic process matters more than the answer. Push groups to say: what specifically did you see that told you it was regression? What behaviors pointed to R1 vs R2? The reasoning is the skill being built.

Style is not fixed. A leader might start with S1, observe that Alex responds well, and shift toward S2 within the same conversation. Diagnose → act → observe → adjust. That responsiveness is the model working as intended.

Tell each group which task to work on. Both checkpoints have two tasks. Assign Task A (leadership/feedback) to half the groups and Task B (technical) to the other half — then compare in debrief.
